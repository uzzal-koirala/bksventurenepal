Who We Are

Building Trust, One Venture at a Time

Real progress comes from connecting industries, not isolating them.

Launched in 2024 in Itahari, B.K.S Venture is a newly founded business group built from day one to span real estate, global trade, logistics and sustainable enterprise. We're early in our journey, but every venture we take on is rooted in the same principles we started with — quality, transparency and long-term value for the communities and partners we work with.
